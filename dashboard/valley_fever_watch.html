Limits worth saying out loud

Checkpoint 2 is a validated proof of concept, not a deployable early-warning system.

  • January–March 2024 coincides with a documented ADHS lab-testing concern (potential false positives). Case counts were not altered; results are also reported excluding those months for fairness.
  • Environmental data helps inconsistently — see the ablation panel above. It even reversed sign across periods for the same county.
  • Pima is the hardest county to forecast — fewer cases means less signal, and the model's improvement over baseline is modest (under 8%) and sometimes negative in sub-periods.
  • PM10 is a dust proxy, not a measurement of airborne Coccidioides spores. PRISM uses a county-centroid estimate, not a true area-weighted average.
  • 2025 figures are provisional and may still be revised by ADHS.
  • Reporting delays are simulated, not independently verified — in Maricopa, a realistic 1-month case-reporting delay alone raises MAE from 132 to about 148.
  • This pipeline is not ready for public-health deployment: no independent real-world validation yet, and Tier 3 (a spatial graph model) was never built.
